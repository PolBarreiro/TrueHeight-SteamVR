# TrueHeight Privacy Notice

Effective: 3 September 2026

TrueHeight does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no
analytics, advertising, account system, or telemetry.

The application reads local SteamVR headset state and stores its settings only
on the user's computer at %APPDATA%\TrueHeight\config.ini. Steam may process
purchase, account, crash-reporting, and platform data under Valve's own privacy
terms; that processing is outside TrueHeight.

Support requests sent through GitHub or Steam are handled by those platforms
under their respective privacy terms.
